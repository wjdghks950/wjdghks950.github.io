From perception to understanding.

I’m Jeonghwan Kim, a Ph.D. candidate at UIUC’s BLENDER Lab, advised by Professor Heng Ji. I am a recipient of the Capital One Ph.D. Fellowship (2026–2027).

I study multimodal foundation models that perceive, ground, and reason over fine-grained visual information. My work connects visual grounding, cross-modal alignment, and knowledge integration to make these systems more accurate and interpretable. Recently, I’ve been extending this work to visually grounded action policies for embodied AI and robotics.
